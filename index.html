1. VMO Teardrop Placement

Attach a $5\times 5\text{ cm}$ pad directly over the VMO teardrop bulge on the inner lower thigh (~5cm above knee joint). Essential for knee stability.

2. Rectus Femoris Placement

Place the large $5\times 10\text{ cm}$ pad high on the upper center thigh near the hip crease. Connect the cable snap marked with the MI logo here.

Mount along the mid-to-outer quad border. Key for building quad width and lateral drive during sprint starts.
